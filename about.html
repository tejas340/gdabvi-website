Who we are

About GDABVI

Helping people find Life Beyond Sight since 1961.

Our mission is to provide services and support that help blind and visually impaired individuals live productive, independent lives with confidence and dignity.

Our vision is that blind and visually impaired people have the tools, technologies, and therapies needed to overcome obstacles.

Our values: empathy, excellence, inclusion, integrity, innovation, joy, respect, and service.

GDABVI takes a whole-person approach to sight loss. Our programs bring together daily living skills, technology training, orientation and mobility, support groups, case management, information and resources, and socialization — because independence is built from many pieces working together.

Since 1961

Our history

  • 1961
    Founded as the Metropolitan Society for the Blind.
  • 1970
    Merged with the Detroit Society for the Prevention of Blindness to become the Greater Detroit Society for the Blind.
  • 1993
    Renamed the Upshaw Institute for the Blind, honoring founding director McAllister Upshaw.
  • 2005
    Became the Greater Detroit Agency for the Blind and Visually Impaired — the GDABVI you know today.

For more than six decades, one thing has never changed: our belief that vision loss is not the end of independence, purpose, or joy. There is Life Beyond Sight.
