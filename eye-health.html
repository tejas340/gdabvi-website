Prevention & awareness

Eye Health

Take power over your eye health to help prevent blindness. Regular comprehensive eye exams and early detection can protect vision.

Please note: this information is educational and is not medical advice. Consult your eye specialist or health provider regarding your specific condition.
Know the signs

Common types of vision loss

Cataracts

Clouding of the eye’s natural lens that can blur or dim vision and is often treatable.

Glaucoma

A group of diseases that damage the optic nerve, often without early symptoms. Regular exams are important.

Macular Degeneration

Age-related changes affecting central vision. Treatment can slow some forms.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetes can damage retinal blood vessels. Blood-sugar management and annual dilated exams reduce risk.

Retinal Detachment

Sudden flashes, floaters, or a curtain over vision require immediate medical attention.

Everyday respect

Courtesy when interacting with people who are blind

  • Speak directly to the person and identify yourself when approaching.
  • Ask before helping; do not assume assistance is needed.
  • When guiding, offer your arm rather than grabbing theirs.
  • Give clear, specific directions and describe obstacles when helpful.
  • Do not pet or distract a working guide dog.
