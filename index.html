How we help

Our whole-person approach meets people wherever they are in their journey with vision loss.

1

Independence Services

Direct services that help individuals and their families adapt to life without sight in a sighted world — from daily living skills to orientation and mobility.

2

Education

Our Education Series covers vision-related health topics including eye conditions, prevention and treatment, healthy living, and safety.

Social Activities
