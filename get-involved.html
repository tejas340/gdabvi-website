Support the mission

Get Involved

The Greater Detroit Agency for the Blind and Visually Impaired relies on generous community support to fulfill its mission of providing no-cost services that foster independence, dignity, and well-being for people of all ages who are blind or visually impaired, across seven Southeast Michigan counties.

When you give, you fund braille and technology training, safe-travel skills, support groups, transportation help, and eye-health education. You fund Life Beyond Sight.
